# Privacy

The Growth Cab GTM Skills run on your machine, inside Claude or from the command line. They collect no data and send nothing to Growth Cab.

- The skills read only the text you paste and the files you point them to.
- The domain check and the lead list MX option make DNS and RDAP lookups for the domains you enter (Cloudflare or Google DNS over HTTPS, rdap.org) and a Spamhaus DBL lookup through your own resolver.
- Nothing else leaves your machine. There is no telemetry, no account and no tracking.
